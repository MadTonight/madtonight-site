Privacy policy

Plain version: I collect your email address so I can send you the MadTonight newsletter. That's it.

What I collect

Your email address, when you sign up. If you arrived from a tagged link, the form also records which link (for example, "instagram") so I can see where signups come from.

How it's used

Only to send you MadTonight, one email a day. The newsletter is sent through Buttondown, which stores the subscriber list and delivers the emails on my behalf. Buttondown may record whether an email was opened or a link was clicked, which I use only to see how the newsletter is doing.

What I don't do

I never sell, rent, or share your email address or data. This site has no ads, analytics, or third-party trackers.

Unsubscribing

You can unsubscribe anytime with one click from the link at the bottom of any email. To have your address deleted, email me and I'll remove it.
